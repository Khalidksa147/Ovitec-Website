Inquiry FAQs

What to expect when you request parts from Ovitec.

How quickly will you respond?

Most inquiries are reviewed the same business day. Complex sourcing for rare exotic parts may take a little longer while we confirm availability.

Do I need a VIN?

A VIN is optional but recommended. It helps us match genuine OEM and aftermarket parts to the exact specification of your vehicle.

Do you ship outside Saudi Arabia?

Yes. We fulfill worldwide from Jeddah with tracked dispatch. Shipping options are confirmed after the part is sourced and quoted.

Can workshops and dealers enquire?

Yes. Select Workshop, Dealer, or Fleet on the form so we can support trade quantities, repeat orders, and compatibility lists.
